Key terms

GRACE and GRACE-FO
Two pairs of satellites, one flown 2002–2017 and one from 2018 onward, that measure small monthly changes in Earth’s gravity. Most of those changes over land come from water moving.
Water storage anomaly
How much more or less water the land holds than in a reference period, in soil, groundwater, lakes and rivers together. Given as a depth of water in millimetres over the area.
GravIS (COST-G and GFZ)
Two published versions of the monthly gravity data, on a 1-degree grid. The main results use COST-G; GFZ is a second version, and the spread between them is reported.
Leakage
Blurring of the gravity signal in and out across the basin edge, because the satellites cannot see detail finer than a few hundred kilometres. The product offers a correction, and results are given with and without it.
GLDAS Noah and TerraClimate
Two estimates of soil water, from a land-surface model and a monthly climate dataset. Subtracting soil water from total storage leaves the remainder.
Remainder
Storage left after soil water is removed: groundwater and surface water together, plus soil water deeper than the soil models reach and the errors of both datasets. This study cannot separate them.
HydroBASINS level 3
A global set of river-basin outlines. The level-3 unit used here holds the whole Lake Chad system.
Breakpoint
The month where a fit of two straight lines changes slope. For total storage it falls in October 2018 (best fits July 2018 to January 2019); after removing GLDAS soil water it falls in April 2019.
